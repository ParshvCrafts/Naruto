Recording Tips (read before collecting data)

Setup: Stand 50–70 cm from camera. Both hands fully visible in frame.

During recording: Slowly drift hands ±5 cm left/right/up/down while holding the sign. This builds position robustness into the model.

  • Tiger (2): Both index fingers pointing straight up at the same height.
  • Ram (7): Left index visibly lower than right index. Pick one convention and stay consistent across all Ram recordings.
  • Serpent (5): Fingers interlaced, pointing downward.
  • Dragon (4): Knuckles interlaced, thumbs tucked. Stand slightly farther from camera.
  • None (13): Record 300+ samples across 3–4 separate takes:
    Take 1 — Both hands relaxed at sides or on desk
    Take 2 — One hand only in frame
    Take 3 — Casual gestures: pointing, waving, typing motion
    Take 4 — Hands partially out of frame
